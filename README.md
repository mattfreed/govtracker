# SF Money Trail

Explore San Francisco public spending through case files, narrated films and downloadable source records. Every case identifies its evidence level, the records behind the findings and the questions that remain open.

- [Read the 215 case files](https://sfgovtracker.com/#cases)
- [Explore the investigation questions and methods](https://sfgovtracker.com/#plan)
- [See which records are still needed](https://sfgovtracker.com/#wanted)
- [Download the investigation plan](FRAUD_DETECTION_PLAN.md)
- [Read the evidence review](EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md)

The November 26, 2026 edition includes 12 proven cases, 6 charged cases, 89 official findings and 108 investigative leads. Organizational relationships and unresolved accounting differences can justify a records request. Evidence of intent, loss or a connection to a particular City payment requires additional support.
